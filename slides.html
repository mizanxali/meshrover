How it works

1At each junction, a rover sends what its camera sees to the base, over the mesh.
2The base asks a vision model on 0G. The rover moves only on that answer, in time. Otherwise it stops.
3Before zone C, an operator approves with World ID on their phone. ENS says who may.
4When the run ends, its log goes to 0G Storage and is anchored on 0G Chain.
